Previously, I worked in [Taiga.io](https://taiga.io), I founded [Initios](https://www.initios.com/), a software company, and co-founded [Parkapp](https://www.parkapp.com/) among other things.

[Agile manifesto](http://agilemanifesto.org/) fan:

I love learning and improving my skills every day. I collaborate on local development groups inside [VigoTech](http://vigotech.org/), active member in [A Industriosa](https://aindustriosa.org) and I'm one of the organizers of [Python Vigo](https://www.python-vigo.es/) and [PyDay Galicia](https://pyday2017.python-vigo.es/).
